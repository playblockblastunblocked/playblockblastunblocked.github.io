What Is Block Blast!?

Block Blast! is a highly popular block puzzle from Hungry Studio that asks you to place pieces cleanly on an 8x8 board and keep the space alive for as long as possible. On this page, the goal is to make that browser session easier to access and easier to understand, especially for players searching for a clean unblocked version.

Block Blast looks simple because the rules are readable, but the tension arrives quickly once the board starts to shrink. Every wasted space changes what will be possible a few turns later.

That balance between accessibility and planning is the reason puzzle players keep returning to it. It is easy to open for a short session, but it also rewards better foresight and board management over time.

How to Play Block Blast!

Place block shapes to clear rows and columns, preserve open space, and keep your run going. The longer you survive, the more the game becomes about planning two or three moves ahead instead of just reacting to the next piece.

The interaction is direct: drag, place, and evaluate the board again. Because the controls are so clean, Block Blast succeeds or fails entirely on puzzle design and player decision-making.

Block Blast! Tips and Strategy

Strong Block Blast play is mostly about space discipline. Leaving flexible openings and avoiding awkward dead zones makes it far easier to survive ugly piece combinations later.

  • Do not fill the center too early unless it creates meaningful clears.
  • Try to leave room for wide or awkward shapes that are hard to place late.
  • Chasing one big combo is rarely worth killing the board layout.
